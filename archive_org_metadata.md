### Description  (plain text)

A complete machine-generated Sanskrit recitation of the Mahābhārata, following the
text of the Bhandarkar Oriental Research Institute (BORI) Critical Edition prepared
by V. S. Sukthankar and colleagues (1933–1966).

The recitation covers all 18 parvans (books) and all 1,995 adhyāyas (chapters) of
the Critical Edition — roughly 232 hours of audio in total. Each chapter is provided
as a single file.

Every chapter file is framed by traditional recitation markers that are NOT part of
the Critical Edition text itself, added to aid navigation:

  • an opening bell;
  • a spoken announcement of the chapter: "atha [ordinal]'dhyāyaḥ" (e.g. "atha
    prathamo'dhyāyaḥ" = "now, the first chapter"), preceded — for the first chapter
    of each parvan — by "śrīman-mahābhārate [parva-name]-parva";
  • the verses of the chapter;
  • a closing colophon in the traditional form "iti śrīman-mahābhārate
    śatasāhasryāṃ saṃhitāyāṃ vaiyāsikyāṃ [parva]-parvaṇi [ordinal]'dhyāyaḥ";
  • a closing bell.

The voice was produced with a neural text-to-speech model trained for metrical
Sanskrit. The recitation aims for clear, metrically-aware delivery of anuṣṭubh and
the other metres of the epic, including its prose (gadya) passages.

Files are named by parvan, chapter number, sub-parvan (upa-parvan), and a short
English description of the chapter's contents — e.g.
"mbh_01_adh_062__Sambhava__story-of-dushyanta.mp3". The English chapter
descriptions and sub-parvan names are drawn from the public-domain Ganguli/Dutt
translations, via the chapter-mapping data at
https://github.com/shreevatsa/mahabharata-align-copy .

This is a synthetic recitation intended for study, listening, and accessibility. It
is not a substitute for a scholarly edition or a traditional trained reciter.
